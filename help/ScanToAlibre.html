ScanToAlibre Help

ScanToAlibre add-on for Alibre Design 27.0.1 and later.

Overview

ScanToAlibre helps you turn a 3D scan into a real, editable Alibre part. You load the scan into a part, clean it up, line it up with the part's planes, and then take what you need from it: sketches of sections and outlines, lofts through a series of sections, and fitted B-spline surfaces.

ScanToAlibre doesn't make solid features itself. It makes sketches, design planes, lofts and surfaces, and you build the part from them with Alibre's own Extrude, Revolve, Hole and so on, so everything stays fully editable. When the part is built, Compare colours the scan by how far it is from the part.

The ScanToAlibre ribbon tab is the workflow, left to right:

  1. Load a scan (STL or PLY) into a part.
  2. Refine it: fewer triangles, and a smoother surface.
  3. Orient it: move, turn and scale it, fit planes and axes to it, and line it up with the part's planes, axes and origin.
  4. Take the shape from it: Section (a slice as a sketch), Section Series (many slices and a loft), Surface (a fitted surface) and Silhouette (an outline as a sketch).
  5. Compare the part you built with the scan.

The scan is saved inside the part (.AD_PRT) when you save the part, with the planes and axes you fitted to it. Only one ScanToAlibre dialog is open at a time: opening one closes another. Each dialog applies its changes straight away, so closing it loses nothing. Every dialog's ? button opens its own section of this page.

All lengths are in millimetres and angles in degrees, whatever the part's units.

Example workflows

A machined part

  1. Open a new part and push Load. Choose the scan's .stl or .ply file.
  2. Push Refine. If the scan is large, Decimate it to 50% or so with Largest change ticked (0.05 mm). Smooth it with Bilateral, which keeps sharp edges. Close the dialog.
  3. Push Orient. Push Select Area and click the part's flat base, then Fit Plane. Do the same for a flat side, and Select Cylinder and Fit Axis for a hole.
  4. In Align (3-2-1): push Scan and click the base plane, push To and click Alibre's XY-Plane, then push Align. Line up the side with the ZX-Plane, then the hole's axis with an Alibre axis or the origin, the same way. The dialog says what's still free to move after each step.
  5. Push Section. Push Alibre Plane and click the XY-Plane, and set an Offset to a height where the slice shows the profile you want. Check the fit in green over the slice in red, then push Create Sketch.
  6. Extrude the sketch with Alibre's Extrude. The Section dialog shows the scan's depth along the plane as a suggestion.
  7. If there are tabs or flanges a slice at one height misses, use Silhouette for the outline seen from above instead.
  8. Push Compare to see where the part still differs from the scan.

A symmetric part

  1. Load, Refine and Orient as above. In Orient, push Find Symmetry: it adds "Symmetry plane 1".
  2. Align the symmetry plane to Alibre's YZ-Plane (Scan, click the plane; To, click YZ-Plane; Align).
  3. In Section or Silhouette, tick Symmetric about a symmetry plane square to this one. The sketch comes out exactly symmetric, with a construction line on the symmetry line and Alibre's Symmetric constraints about it.
  4. In Surface, push Detect to tick the world planes the scan is symmetric about, so the surface is mirrored exactly.

An organic shape

  1. Load, Refine and Orient so the shape's length runs along an Alibre axis.
  2. Push Section Series. Pick a plane square to the length (an Alibre plane, or one of the scan's box planes). The list fills with 10 planes from end to end.
  3. Leave Create Loft, Fit Spline and Create Guide Curves ticked, and push Create Loft. ScanToAlibre makes a sketch at each plane and a loft boss through them.
  4. If it warns that two neighbouring sections don't overlap, delete the smaller one from the list or add planes between them, so the shape changes slowly from one to the next.

A freeform top

  1. Load, Refine and Orient so the freeform face looks up along Z.
  2. Push Surface. Push Smooth Area and click the freeform face; add any missed areas with Brush. Check the green grid follows the scan and the deviations are small.
  3. Push Create Surface. It's inserted into the part as a design surface.
  4. Make the outline with Silhouette on the XY-Plane, then extrude it with Alibre's Extrude To Geometry, choosing the surface.

Checking the part

  1. With the part built (or partly built), push Compare.
  2. Green is within the Tolerance; blues are scan inside the part (too much part), yellow to red scan outside it (too little), and grey isn't modelled yet.
  3. Push Probe and click the scan to read the distance at a point.
  4. After changing the part, close Compare and open it again: the part is read when it opens.

The ScanToAlibre tab

The ScanToAlibre ribbon tab holds, in order:

Show Scan
Shows or hides the part's scan. See Show Scan.
Load
Reads a scan into the part. See Load.
Refine, Orient, Section, Section Series, Surface, Silhouette, Compare
Open their dialogs, described below.
Help
Opens this page at the top, in your web browser.
About
Shows the add-on's version. See About.

Load works in parts. Show Scan and the dialogs need the part to have a scan, and are greyed until it does. Help and About work everywhere.

Show Scan

A toggle. On, the scan stays drawn while you use Alibre's own commands, such as Measure, and a Scan tab appears in the Design Explorer. Off, the scan is drawn only while one of ScanToAlibre's dialogs is open. It's the one control for the scan: once you've turned it off, the scan stays hidden.

When Show Scan is first turned on, Alibre may report one "Object reference not set to an instance of an object" error. Close the message; Alibre carries on.

Scan tab

The Scan tab holds a tree. Under Scan Geometry are the planes and axes fitted to the scan in Orient.

Click a plane or axis
Draws it in yellow over the model.
Rename (right-click, or F2)
Edits the name in place. A symmetry plane stays one when renamed.
Delete (right-click, or the Delete key)
Removes it at once, with no prompt. Orient's Undo brings it back.

Load

Asks for a scan file and reads it into the open part. If the part already has a scan, it asks before replacing it.

STL
Binary or text.
PLY mesh
Text or binary, with faces.
PLY point cloud
Points with normals are made into a mesh as they load. Areas the scanner didn't see stay open. A cloud without normals is refused.

The scan is saved inside the part when you save it. Alibre sees the part as changed after a Load (a parameter named ScanToAlibre counts the changes), so Save lights up.

The scan in the view

The scan is drawn in light blue, hidden by the part's solid. Large scans are drawn simplified while you work.

F
Switches between the simplified scan and full detail.
Left-click on the scan
Picks a point, marked with crosshairs (when no dialog is using clicks).
C
Clears the picked points.

Alibre's own commands, such as Measure, stop ScanToAlibre's view, so the scan disappears until you reopen a dialog, unless Show Scan is on.

Refine

Changes the scan's mesh straight away. Undo takes back up to five changes. The choices are remembered until Alibre closes.

Scan
The scan's name and how many triangles it has now.

Decimate: fewer triangles

Fewer triangles make everything faster. Decimating merges small triangles where the surface is flat (quadric edge collapse).

Triangles
Decimate to this many triangles.
Percent of now
Decimate to this share of the triangles there are now (default 50%).
Largest change (mm)
When ticked, stops before the surface would move further than this (default 0.05 mm), even if that leaves more triangles than asked for.
Keep open edges
Keeps the edges of holes and of the scan's borders where they are (default on).
Decimate
Does it.

Smooth

Method
Laplacian is simple and shrinks the shape a little. Taubin keeps the size. Bilateral (the default) keeps sharp edges, best for machined parts.
Passes
How many times to smooth (default 5). More passes smooth more.
Strength (0 to 1)
How far each pass moves the surface (default 0.5).
Keep open edges
Keeps the edges of holes and borders where they are (default on).
Smooth
Does it.
Status line
What the last change did and how long it took.
Undo
Takes back the last change: Refine's, or Orient's moves and fitted planes. Up to five.
Close
Closes the dialog. Changes are already made.

Orient

Moves, turns and scales the scan, fits planes and axes to it, and lines it up with the part's own planes, axes and origin. While Orient is open, the scan's fitted box is drawn: its edges, its three centre planes (A along the longest direction in red, then B in green, C in blue), its axes and its centre. Fitted planes are orange, the chosen one yellow, and the selected area orange.

Three buttons say what a click on the scan does: Select Area or Select Cylinder (grow a selection), Scan (choose a scan plane, axis or point) and To (choose Alibre geometry). They stay down until you push them again or push another; with none down, clicks go to Alibre.

Scan
The scan's name and size.

Move (mm)

X, Y, Z
How far to move the scan along each axis.
Move
Moves it. This starts the alignment over.
Centre on the Origin
Moves the fitted box's centre to the origin.

Rotate

About
The axis to turn about: X, Y or Z.
by ... degrees
The angle to turn by.
through
Turn about the origin, or about the scan's centre.
Rotate
Turns it. This starts the alignment over.

Scale

Factor
The same factor every way, such as 25.4 for a scan made in inches.
through
Scale about the origin, or about the scan's centre.
Scale
Scales it. This starts the alignment over.

Fit a plane, an axis or a symmetry plane

Select Area
While down, a click on the scan selects the flat area round it, and a click on a selected area takes it away.
Select Cylinder
While down, a click selects the cylinder round it, outside or a hole.
within ... degrees and ... mm
How far a triangle may tilt (default 10 degrees) and lie (default 0.2 mm) from the area's fitted plane or cylinder and still join it.
Selected
How many triangles are selected.
Clear
Empties the selection.
Fit Plane
Adds a plane fitted to the selection, named Plane 1, Plane 2 and so on.
Fit Axis
Adds the selected cylinder's centre line, named Axis 1 and so on, with its diameter and whether it's a hole.
Find Symmetry
Adds the scan's best plane of symmetry, "Symmetry plane n". Push it again for the next best. It says "No symmetry found" when under 30% of the scan matches its mirror image.
Mid Plane
With a plane, axis or point chosen (Scan down), push Mid Plane and click a second of the same kind. Adds the plane halfway between two planes, or square to the line joining two axes or two points through its middle, named "Mid plane n".

Align (3-2-1)

Lines the scan up with Alibre geometry one step at a time. Each step turns and slides the scan only in the ways earlier steps left free. A plane goes onto a plane, an axis onto an axis or a plane, and a point onto a point.

Step line
What's still free to move or turn.
Scan
While down, a click chooses one of the scan's planes, axes or its centre. Click near a plane's outline or cross to reach one behind another; clicking the chosen plane again takes the next one behind.
Scan feature
The chosen plane, axis or point.
Delete
Removes the chosen fitted plane or axis (not the box's own).
Delete All
After asking, removes every fitted plane and axis, symmetry and mid planes too. Undo brings them back.
To
While down, a click on an Alibre design plane, axis or point makes it the target.
Target
The chosen Alibre geometry.
Axis onto a plane
When an axis goes onto a plane: square to it or lying in it.
Flip
Turns the scan's plane or axis the other way round before aligning.
Start Over
Frees every direction again, keeping the scan where it is.
Align
Moves the scan to line the chosen feature up with the target. A step that would line up nothing new is refused.
Status line
What the last action did.
Undo
Takes back moves, fitted planes and axes, deletions and Refine's changes, up to five.
Close
Closes the dialog. The scan, its fitted planes and axes are saved with the part; the alignment's progress lasts until the part closes.

Section

Slices the scan with a plane, fits lines and arcs to the slice and makes an Alibre sketch of them. The plane is drawn yellow, the slice red and the fit green. You can pick another plane and make another sketch without closing the dialog.

Scan
The scan's name and size.

Plane

Alibre Plane
Push, then click an Alibre design plane or a flat face of the part. The button comes up once a plane is chosen.
Scan Plane
Push, then click one of the scan's box or fitted planes. An Alibre design plane is made for it when the sketch is created.
Plane
The chosen plane.
Offset
Moves the slice along the plane's normal (mm). With an offset, a design plane is made there for the sketch. A plane lying on a flat area of the scan slices badly; offset it a little.

Fit lines and arcs

Tolerance
How far the fitted lines and arcs may be from the slice (default 0.05 mm). Also the distance within which arc centres count as concentric.
Horizontal and vertical
Lines within 2 degrees of the sketch's horizontal or vertical are made so.
Tangent
Joins within 3 degrees of tangent are made tangent.
Concentric
Arcs whose centres are within the tolerance are made concentric.
Round radii to
Arc and circle sizes are rounded to 0.1 mm (default) or 0.01 mm and dimensioned, or left as they are (Off).
Symmetric about a symmetry plane square to this one
Makes the fit exactly symmetric about the first symmetry plane (from Find Symmetry or Mid Plane in Orient, or one Surface's Detect found) that's square to the sketch plane. The result line says which plane and how far apart the two sides are.

Sketch Options

Create Raw Sketch
Puts the slice itself in the sketch, as construction lines when the fitted sketch is made too, otherwise as ordinary lines.
Create Fitted Sketch
Puts the fitted lines and arcs in the sketch with their constraints and dimensions.
Fit arcs
Unticked, the fit uses lines only.

Section

Result
How many loops and runs the slice has, how many lines and arcs fit them, and the largest distance from the slice.
Depth
How far the scan reaches either side of the plane: a suggested depth for Alibre's Extrude.
Status line
What the last action did.
Create Sketch
Makes the sketch, "Scan Section n", and clears Alibre's selection. Greyed when neither sketch option is ticked.
Close
Closes the dialog.

Section Series

Slices the scan at a series of planes offset from one plane and makes a sketch at each, and with Create Loft, a loft through them. Every slice is drawn thin red, the chosen row's slice thicker with its fit in green, and the guide curves dark green.

Scan
The scan's name and size.

Plane

Pick Alibre Plane
Push, then click an Alibre design plane or flat face.
Pick Scan Plane
Push, then click one of the scan's box or fitted planes.
Plane
The chosen plane. Picking one fills the list again.

Series

First, Last
The first and last offsets along the plane's normal (mm). They start at the scan's extent less 2% at each end.
Count
How many planes (default 10).
Make
Fills the list with Count planes evenly from First to Last.
List
One row per plane: its offset, its number, its loops and what the fit makes. Double-click a row or press F2 to edit its offset. Offsets must go up from row to row.
Add
Adds a plane halfway between the chosen row and the next.
Delete
Removes the chosen rows.

Loft

Create Loft
Follows each closed loop from slice to slice and makes a loft boss through each run of matching slices (default on). The result line names the runs.
Fit Spline
Each slice becomes a closed spline with the same number of points as the others, starting at matching places (default).
Use lines and Arcs
Each slice is fitted with lines and arcs split so every slice has the same pieces in the same places. A run that can't be matched falls back to splines, and says so.
Create Guide Curves
Adds 3D sketch splines through matching points of the slices, used as the loft's guides (default on).

Fit and Sketch Options

Tolerance, Horizontal and vertical, Tangent, Concentric, Round radii to, Create Raw Sketch, Create Fitted Sketch and Fit arcs work as in Section. Sections that are lofted get their ends joined but no other constraints or dimensions, so Alibre's solver can't move them.

Result
The runs of matching slices and what each fit makes.
Status line
What the last action did.
Create Sketches / Create Loft
Makes a design plane and sketch, "Scan Series n.k", for each plane, and with Create Loft the loft "Scan Series n loft r" for each run. If neighbouring sections don't overlap, it warns and asks before lofting. If Alibre refuses the guides, the loft is made without them.
Close
Closes the dialog.

Surface

Fits a B-spline surface to areas of the scan and inserts it into the part as a design surface, to extrude up to with Alibre's own commands. The selection is drawn orange, the surface as a green grid and its edge yellow. It's fitted again whenever the selection or a choice changes.

Scan
The scan's name and size.

Select

Smooth Area
While down, a click takes the area round it over which neighbouring triangles bend less than the Angle. Clicking a selected triangle takes its area away.
Brush
While down, a click takes the joined triangles within the Brush radius.
Clear
Empties the selection.
Angle
For Smooth Area (default 15 degrees).
Brush radius
For Brush (default 3 mm).
Selected
How many triangles are selected.

Base plane

The surface is a height over this plane, so a selection that folds back over it is refused.

Auto
The plane fitted to the selection (default), turned square to any ticked symmetry planes.
XY, YZ, ZX
That world plane.

Symmetry

XY, YZ, ZX
Mirrors the selection across that world plane, so the surface is exactly symmetric. At most two; the base plane's own is greyed.
Detect
Scores the scan's symmetry about each world plane as it lies, and ticks those with at least 80% matching (at most the best two). Align the scan's symmetry plane with a world plane in Orient first.
Scores
Detect's score for each plane, such as "YZ: 94%".

Surface perimeter

Use scan projection
Trims the surface to the whole scan's outline seen along the base plane's normal, holes filled.
Use scan bounding rectangle
An untrimmed rectangle round that outline, plus 2%.
Use scan bounding box
An untrimmed rectangle round Orient's fitted box seen along the normal, plus 2%.
Use selection
Trims the surface to the selection's outline (default).
Offset
Makes the perimeter bigger every way by this much (mm, default 0). The surface carries on smoothly past the scan.

Fit

Tolerance
Control points are added until 99% of the selection is within this (default 0.05 mm), at most 40 each way.
Smoothing
0 to 1 (default 0.2). Higher makes a smoother surface that follows the scan's noise less.
Result
The control points each way, the share within the tolerance, and the largest and RMS distances.
Status line
What the last action did.
Create Surface
Inserts the surface into the part as a design surface.
Close
Closes the dialog.

Silhouette

Makes a sketch of the scan's outline seen along a plane's normal, with every separate piece and every through-hole. It catches tabs and flanges that a slice at one height misses. The dialog is Section's, with one more field.

Scan
The scan's name and size.
Alibre Plane, Scan Plane, Offset
Choose the sketch plane, as in Section. The offset moves the sketch plane; the outline is the same at any offset.
Tolerance, Horizontal and vertical, Tangent, Concentric, Round radii to, Symmetric
Fit the outline with lines and arcs, as in Section.
Smallest hole
Holes and pieces smaller than this across are left out (default 1 mm), such as pinholes and gaps in the scan.
Create Raw Sketch, Create Fitted Sketch, Fit arcs
As in Section.
Result
How many loops the outline has, how many lines and arcs fit them, and the largest distance.
Depth
The scan's extent along the normal: a suggested depth for Alibre's Extrude.
Status line
What the last action did.
Create Sketch
Makes the sketch, "Scan Silhouette n".
Close
Closes the dialog.

Compare

Colours the scan by how far it is from the part as built so far. The part is read when the dialog opens; open it again after changing the part. With no solid yet, it says there's nothing to compare.

Scan
The scan's name and size.

Colours

Tolerance
Within this either way is green (default 0.1 mm).
Range
How far to look for the part (default 1 mm). Scan further than this from it is grey, "not modelled". Widening the range searches again; the other changes only recolour.
Legend
The colours and their edges: dark blue, blue and light blue where the scan is inside the part, green within the tolerance, yellow, orange and red where it's outside, and grey not compared.

Statistics

Statistics
How many points were compared and what share, the largest distance outside and inside, the RMS, and the share within the tolerance.
Probe
While down, a click on the scan shows the distance there, or "not modelled", and marks the point.
Status
The probe's reading, or what the dialog is doing.
Close
Closes the dialog.

About

Shows ScanToAlibre's version and the version of Alibre Design it's running in.

OK
